About Near Ambulance Service

Near Ambulance Service is a fast and reliable emergency response platform designed to connect patients with nearby ambulances in critical situations. Our goal is to reduce response time and save lives.

The system allows admins to manage drivers, monitor ambulance status, and ensure availability across locations.

Key Features 🚑

  • 📍 Find nearby ambulances in real-time
  • ⏱️ Faster emergency response
  • 👨‍✈️ Verified ambulance drivers
  • 📞 Easy and quick booking
  • 🛡️ Secure and reliable service

Our Mission 🎯

To provide timely and efficient emergency medical transportation by leveraging technology and a trusted ambulance network.

About

The Near Ambulance Service is a web-based application that connects users with nearby ambulances during medical emergencies.

Near Ambulance Service is dedicated to providing prompt and reliable emergency medical transportation services. Our mission is to ensure that individuals in need of urgent medical care have access to professional ambulance services in their vicinity.

With a network of skilled drivers and well-equipped ambulances, we strive to deliver exceptional service and support during critical moments. Our commitment to safety, efficiency, and compassion sets us apart as a trusted partner in emergency medical transportation.

Our Services

  • 24/7 Emergency Ambulance Services
  • Advanced Life Support (ALS) and Basic Life Support (BLS) Ambulances
  • Trained and Certified Medical Personnel
  • Real-time Location Tracking for Quick Response

Key Features

Automatic Location Detection

Automatically detects the user's location using the HTML Geolocation API.

Distance Sorting

Sorts available ambulances based on proximity, ensuring the nearest one is displayed first.

SOS Call Button

Provides a quick access button to call emergency services (108) with a single tap
